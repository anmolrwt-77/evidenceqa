1. Question: Give one example of a prohibited AI practice under the EU AI Act.

I found it in: eu_ai_act_2024_1689.pdf — article/heading: Article 5 Prohibited AI Practices  

Short answer in my words: AI systems are banned if they secretly or deliberately manipulate people into making decisions they otherwise wouldn’t make, especially when this could cause significant harm.



1. Question: What does the EU AI Act require regarding AI literacy of staff?

I found it in: eu_ai_act_2024_1689.pdf — article/heading: Article 4 AI literacy

  Short answer in my words: Providers and deployers must take measures so their staff have enough AI literacy to use AI systems properly and safely.



1. Question: Name one risk that NIST associates specifically with generative AI.

I found it in: nist_genai_profile.pdf — section/heading: Overview of Risks Unique to or Exacerbated by GAI

  Short answer in my words: generative AI should not make it easier to access or create harmful information or designs related to chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear weapons.
